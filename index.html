Rigorous safeguards

Quality Assurance Controls We Deploy

⛨
Contributor Qualification Tests

Mandatory language, comprehension, and domain testing prior to live work assignment.

⛨
Gold-Standard Calibration Tasks

Hidden benchmark questions inserted randomly within live batches to measure annotator accuracy.

⛨
Dual-Pass & Independent Second Review

Senior leads audit a defined percentage of every batch independently with blind validation.

⛨
Standardized Error Categorization

Mistakes are cataloged by severity (critical, major, minor) and reported back to clients transparently.

⛨
Inter-Annotator Agreement

Continuous Cohen's Kappa / Fleiss Kappa calculation across parallel contributors to prevent rubric drift.

⛨
Targeted Retraining & Rapid Substitution

Underperforming contributors are immediately quarantined, retrained, or replaced without pausing delivery.
